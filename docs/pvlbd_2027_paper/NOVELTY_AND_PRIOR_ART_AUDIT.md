## 2. Strict Three-Way Attribution of SDG-MH Components

To prevent academic overclaiming in PVLDB 2027, we classify every component of SDG-MH into **(A) Existing Known Methods**, **(B) Mathematically New Technical Results**, and **(C) Systems & Engineering Integration**:

### Category A: Existing Known Methods (Must Cite Prior Art, Do NOT Claim as Novel Mathematics)
1. **Exact zCDP Composition & $(\varepsilon, \delta)$ Tail Conversion**: Direct application of Bun & Steinke (TCC 2016) and Mironov (CSF 2017).
2. **Exact Discrete Gaussian Sampler $\mathcal{N}_{\mathbb{Z}}(0, \sigma^2)$**: Direct implementation of Canonne, Kamath, & Steinke (NeurIPS 2020) via Discrete Laplace rejection sampling.
3. **Daily Independent Gaussian Sealing & Isotropic Budget Split $\eta_k = C_k^2 / \sum_j C_j^2$**: Adding independent noise to daily partitions is the classical baseline in continual counting ("Simple II" in Chan et al. 2011; diagonal strategy in Li et al. 2015), and setting $\rho_k \propto C_k^2$ to minimize $\sum_k C_k^2 / (2\rho_k)$ is a standard Cauchy–Schwarz / Lagrange multiplier identity.
4. **Per-User Contribution Clipping Bias–Variance Trade-off ($C_r$ Pareto Curve)**: Direct empirical instantiation of Amin et al. (ICML 2019) applied to longitudinal LLM request telemetry.

### Category B: Mathematically New Technical Results (Genuine Theoretical Contributions of This Paper)
1. **Multi-Horizon User-Time Adjacency (`(u, I)`-zCDP Commitment Curve $\bar\rho(h)$)**:
   - *Prior Art Gap*: Kellaris et al. (PVLDB 2014) enforce privacy at only a **single** window length $w$ (allowing budget $\rho_w$ to recycle every $w$ days so cumulative loss over $h = kw$ grows at rate $\rho_w / w$, which breaches tighter long-horizon caps). Conversely, multi-epoch matrix mechanisms (Choquette-Choo et al. 2023) fix a single terminal horizon $T$.
   - *What is New*: Formalizing simultaneous privacy constraints across **all contiguous user-time intervals $I = [s, s+h-1]$ of every length $h \in \mathbb{N}^+$** via a Multi-Horizon commitment curve $\bar\rho(h)$ combining tight short-horizon anchors ($h \in \{1, 7, 30, 90, 365\}$) with a bounded asymptotic affine growth rate $\bar\rho(h) = a_{\text{tail}} + \beta_{\text{tail}} h$ for $h \ge H_0 = 365$.
2. **Exact Data-Dependency Window Intersection Formula $n_f(s, h; \phi_f)$ ($L = W_f + h - 1$) & Non-Concave Affine-Tail 575-Day Finite-to-Infinite Reduction Theorem**:
   - *Prior Art Gap*: Periodic release schedules for non-additive multi-day windows $B_f(t_k) = [t_k - W_f + 1, t_k]$ have overlapping data dependencies across user intervals $I$. Naive accounting that counts only release timestamps $t_k \in I$ undercounts true zCDP exposure by up to **$5\times$** (proved via counterexample $W_f=30, S_f=7, h=1$). Furthermore, practical multi-horizon curves (`Original_Planner` and `Week1_v2`) are **provably non-concave** in zCDP space, invalidating any concavity-based horizon reduction.
   - *What is New*:
     1. Closed-form intersection count $n_f(s, h; \phi_f) = 1 + \lfloor (W_f + h - 2 - (( \phi_f - s) \bmod S_f)) / S_f \rfloor$ for $\Delta \le W_f + h - 2$ (Theorem 1).
     2. Exact $P = \operatorname{lcm}(7, 7, 30) = 210$-day periodic shift identity $L_{\max}(h + P) = L_{\max}(h) + P \cdot \text{slope}$ (Theorem 2).
     3. **Non-Concave Affine-Tail Infinite-Horizon Reduction (Theorem 3)**: Proving that for *any* commitment curve that is affine for $h \ge H_0 = 365$ (regardless of non-concavity on $[1, H_0]$), verifying $L_{\max}(h) \le \bar\rho(h)$ on the finite prefix $h \in \{1, \dots, H_0 + P = 575\}$ plus the linear tail slope condition $d + \sum_f x_f / S_f \le \beta_{\text{tail}}$ is **necessary and sufficient** for security over all $h \in [1, \infty)$ and all $t \in [1, \infty)$.
3. **Phase-Equivalence Class Reduction & Verifiable Directed-Rounding Lagrangian Dual Lower Bound (`DECIMAL100_DIRECTED_ROUNDING`)**:
   - *What is New*: Reducing $7 \times 7 \times 30 = 1,470$ phase combinations via translational shift invariance to $1 \times 7 \times 30 = 210$ canonical equivalence classes, coupled with an exact box-constrained Lagrangian dual lower bound evaluated in 100-digit `Decimal` directed rounding (`ROUND_FLOOR` / `ROUND_CEILING`, Theorem 4) that eliminates IEEE-754 subtractive cancellation vulnerabilities (which we proved can overestimate weak duality by $+0.2166$ under standard `float64` $(1 - 10^{-14})$ deductions).

### Category C: Systems & Engineering Integration Contributions
1. **Unified Atomic Ledger Admission & Conservative Floating-Point Drift Bound**:
   - Two-phase `RESERVED -> COMMITTED` transactional batch gate (`admit_and_commit_batch`) with `strict_zero_tol=True` and a closed-form worst-case IEEE-754 $\varepsilon$-drift bound $\Delta\varepsilon \le 3.22 \times 10^{-11}$.
2. **Bounded-State Rolling Distinct Engine (`RollingDistinctState`)**:
   - Enforces exact window-level per-user $k$-task contribution capping across sliding 7/30/90-day windows ($\Delta^2 = m + 2k$) in $O(W_{\max})$ active state without raw log retention, avoiding the multiplicative bias of pan-private sketches (Mir et al. 2011) and the $\tilde{O}(\sqrt{T/W})$ continual distinct blowup (Ghazi et al. 2023) by aligning releases to planned periodic calendars.

## 3. Exact Meaning of *"No Epochs, No Expiry, Perpetual Analytics"*

A critical source of reviewer misunderstanding in continual DP papers is conflating **perpetual system operation** with **bounded constant lifetime user-level privacy**. Our paper must state the following distinction with mathematical precision in Section 1 and Section 2:

1. **What *"No Epochs, No Expiry"* DOES Mean**:
   - **No Calendar Epochs (No Hard Cutoffs or Silent Resets)**: Unlike epoch-partitioned systems (`Epoch90`) that either reject all queries once an epoch budget is exhausted or insecurely reset privacy counters at calendar boundaries (creating a $2\times$ boundary spike across days $[89, 91]$), SDG-MH operates continuously without calendar boundaries or service blackouts.
   - **No Utility Expiry (Stationary Noise Scale)**: Unlike $1/t^2$ budget decay (`LifetimeDecay`), where daily noise $\sigma_t \propto t$ diverges to $\infty$ so analytics become useless on long-running deployments, SDG-MH maintains constant, time-invariant noise variances $(\sigma_r, \sigma_f, \sigma_7, \sigma_{30}, \sigma_{90})$ indefinitely.
2. **What *"No Epochs, No Expiry"* DOES NOT Mean**:
   - **It Does NOT Grant Bounded Constant Infinite-Horizon Privacy ($\lim_{h \to \infty} \bar\rho(h) < \infty$) to a Perpetual Daily Participant**: By fundamental information-theoretic lower bounds for continual release (Dwork et al. 2010; Chan et al. 2011; Kellaris et al. 2014), any mechanism that releases fresh daily statistics with bounded error $\sigma_t \le \sigma_{\max} < \infty$ must incur cumulative privacy loss $\rho(h) = \Omega(h)$ for a user who actively changes their data every day over an $h$-day span.
   - Under SDG-MH, a user's cumulative zCDP exposure over any contiguous participation window of length $h$ is bounded by $\bar\rho(h)$, which satisfies tight sub-linearly stepped caps over short-to-annual horizons ($h \in \{1, 7, 30, 90, 365\}$) and grows linearly at the explicitly governed rate $\beta_{\text{tail}} \approx 7.96 \times 10^{-4}\text{ day}^{-1}$ for $h \ge 365$.

## 4. PVLDB 2027 Novelty Audit: Academic Rejection Risks (Critical / Major / Minor)

### Critical Risks (Would Cause Immediate Reject If Mishandled in Text)
- **Risk C1 — Overclaiming Daily Independent Gaussian or $\eta_k = C_k^2/\sum C_j^2$ as a Novel Mechanism**:
  - *Why Reviewers Reject*: PVLDB reviewers familiar with Chan et al. (2011), Kellaris et al. (2014), and Li et al. (2015) know that adding independent Gaussian noise to daily counts ("Simple II") is the textbook baseline. If the paper frames daily Gaussian sealing itself as an algorithmic breakthrough rather than an intentional architectural choice that is **optimal specifically under linear asymptotic multi-horizon slope constraints ($\bar\rho(h) \propto h$)**, reviewers will reject for lack of novelty.
  - *Mitigation in Manuscript*: Explicitly credit prior art for daily independent Gaussian & Cauchy–Schwarz allocation; frame Contribution 1 around the **Multi-Horizon User-Time Adjacency model** and the theoretical & empirical crossover explaining *why* binary trees (`Tree-2l`, `Tree-U`) and $w$-event (`W-BD-30`) lose their advantage when simultaneously constrained across all $h \in [1, 420]$ under a linear tail slope.
- **Risk C2 — Misleading Title/Claims Around "Perpetual Privacy"**:
  - *Why Reviewers Reject*: If a reviewer interprets "Perpetual Analytics" as claiming $\sup_{h \ge 1} \rho(h) < \infty$ with constant noise, they will flag a violation of Dinur–Nissim (2003) / Dwork et al. (2010).
  - *Mitigation in Manuscript*: Dedicate a formal Remark in Section 2 explicitly stating $\bar\rho(h) = \Theta(h)$ for $h \ge 365$ and contrasting bounded-interval user-time privacy against finite-total user-level DP.

### Major Risks (Must Be Pre-Empted with Honest Scope & Data)
- **Risk M1 — Modest Empirical Magnitude of Phase Optimization (`D` vs. `B`) and Non-Significant `30d` Gain**:
  - *Why Reviewers Challenge*: Across 210 canonical phases under `Week1_v2` and `Original`, phase shifting ($\phi = (0, 4, 0)$) reduces the abstract objective by `1.04%–1.12%`, improves `90d` Distinct MAPE by `-3.19%` to `-3.75%` ($p_{\text{holm}} < 10^{-4}$) and `7d` Distinct MAPE by `-0.89%` to `-0.96%` ($p_{\text{holm}} \le 10^{-4}$), but yields a **statistically non-significant** difference on `30d` Distinct ($p_{\text{holm}} = 1.000$) and `0%` phase gain on `Default` weights ($D \equiv B$), while increasing daily additive RMSE by `+0.38%` to `+0.51%`.
  - *Mitigation in Manuscript*: Report all negative and non-significant cells upfront in Section 6; position joint planning not as a magic multiplier on smooth curves, but as an **automated, certified feasibility & conflict-resolution engine** that prevents naive schedule collisions (which reduce utility by `29.98%` in Group A) and extracts the exact Pareto frontier without human tuning.
- **Risk M2 — Absence of Toeplitz / Matrix Factorization (`Fichtenberger et al. 2023`, `Denisov et al. 2022`) in Empirical Plot**:
  - *Why Reviewers Challenge*: Reviewers may ask how Toeplitz factorization compares to `Tree-2l` and `SDG-MH`.
  - *Mitigation in Manuscript*: Discuss Fichtenberger et al. (2023) and Denisov et al. (2022) rigorously in Section 2 and Section 7: explain that Toeplitz mechanisms optimize **all-prefix sums** under a **single** participation bound ($1$ or $k$), where their sensitivity grows as $\Theta(\sqrt{\log T})$, whereas under a Multi-Horizon linear rate $\bar\rho(h) \propto h$, independent daily noise has zero cross-day sensitivity coupling ($\rho(h) = h \cdot d$) and achieves the exact single-horizon information lower bound on 1-day queries.
- **Risk M3 — Synthetic Workload Heavy-Tail Clipping Bias Dominance at $w = 90$**:
  - *Why Reviewers Challenge*: At $w = 90$ on Requests ($C_r = 50$), deterministic clipping bias (`-1437.7/day`, `-129.4k` over 90 days) dominates mechanism noise so `Tree-2l` (`126186.5`) and `SDG-MH` (`126664.8`) have overlapping confidence intervals (`Tree-2l` mean is slightly lower by `0.38%`).
  - *Mitigation in Manuscript*: Explicitly highlight in Section 6.5 that `Tree-2l` has a slightly lower mean at $w=90$ Requests (`126186.5` vs. `126664.8`, statistically indistinguishable within CI) because 90-day error is clipping-bias dominated, whereas on low-bias `Failures` ($w=1, 30, 90$) and short-window `Requests` ($w=1, 7$), `SDG-MH` strictly dominates.

- **Risk m2 — Numerical Certificate vs. Symbolic Proof**: `DECIMAL100_DIRECTED_ROUNDING` certifies `rel_gap <= 7.45e-9` conditional on the `float64` commitment vector `rho_bars`; it is strictly labeled `NEAR_GLOBAL_OPTIMAL_CERTIFIED` (`is_math_global_optimal = False`).
